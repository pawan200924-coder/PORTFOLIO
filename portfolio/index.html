From Forecourt Signage
to Full Brand Systems

I design for the real world, not just the screen. Eight years of building branding systems for petroleum retail networks taught me to think in fabrication tolerances, material specs, and install constraints — not just layouts. Every canopy, monolith, and showroom fascia I've designed has had to survive weather, vendors, and site conditions.

At Ambujam Advertising, I work directly from client engineering documents — IOCL and EPCM-issued drawings — translating technical specifications into production-ready artwork: correct bleeds, Pantone-matched colour, and material call-outs that fabrication teams can build from without guesswork.

I've coordinated branding rollouts across 50+ retail locations for IndianOil, HP, Bharat Petroleum, and Nayara — managing the full lifecycle from concept through client approval, print production, and dispatch, to final on-site installation.
